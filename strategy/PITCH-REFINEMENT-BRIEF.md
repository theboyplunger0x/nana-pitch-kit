# Pitch refinement brief

## Objective

Turn the current deck into a sharper external pitch for investors and partners without weakening the product model that the team has already established.

The current v2 is a strong narrative and visual baseline. Refinement should focus on hierarchy, rhythm, compression and proof—not on changing the core company thesis.

## The one-sentence company thesis

**Nana is an agentic financial account that allows people to act independently within a trusted, programmable network of relationships and permissions.**

For the initial audience:

**The agentic wallet for seniors and people with limited mobility.**

Brand promise:

**Independence through voice. Protection when it matters.**

## Non-negotiable product truths

1. Nana is the financial product, not a voice wrapper around a crypto wallet.
2. Voice is the interface; the agent turns intent into a prepared action.
3. The end user keeps authority and explicitly confirms consequences.
4. The responsible person intervenes by exception, not by default.
5. The agent's execution authority must be bounded, transparent and revocable.
6. Nana owns the agent, product experience, Family Graph, Protection Engine, policies, orchestration, brand and user relationship.
7. Wallet infrastructure, stablecoins, settlement, KYC and local payment rails can be supplied by partners.
8. Core protection stays free. Nana Family monetizes expanded coordination, not safety.
9. Seniors and limited mobility are the wedge, not the permanent boundary of the company.

## Evidence versus hypotheses

### Evidence already used

- A functioning voice-to-transfer flow with explicit confirmation and receipt.
- Seven agentic transfers, as reported in the original team deck.
- Second place in the Tether WDK hackathon, as reported by the team.
- Product code and demo in the original repository.
- Global ageing and disability context from official WHO sources.

### Hypotheses that must stay labeled

- Responsible-led customer acquisition.
- Nana Family willingness to pay and exact pricing unit.
- Financial-activity revenue share.
- B2B2C licensing or distribution.
- Protection thresholds and escalation policies.
- The best infrastructure combination for each market.
- Circle/Arc as an evaluation path rather than a committed dependency.

## Biggest opportunities for the next design pass

1. **Increase emotional contrast.** The problem slide can feel more human before the deck becomes architectural.
2. **Make proof more concrete.** Use the demo screenshots, repository activity or a concise product video frame rather than adding unsupported metrics.
3. **Reduce repetition.** Dual user, exception logic and protection engine are related; transitions should make them feel cumulative.
4. **Clarify the Family Graph.** It is strategically important but currently implicit in the pitch. It may deserve a visual moment if room allows.
5. **Unify team photography.** Current images are usable placeholders but not a consistent portrait system.
6. **Keep the infrastructure slide product-first.** Partner logos or chains must not become the identity of Nana.
7. **Preserve the landing world's warmth.** The deck should feel accessible and human even when discussing permissions or rails.
