## Decisions already made

- Nana is a full financial product with infrastructure abstraction.
- The product has an end user and a responsible user.
- Normal activity should not require family approval.
- The responsible intervenes only on exceptions or policy crossings.
- Delegated agent authority must be limited and revocable.
- Family Graph is a core product layer.
- Safety is included in Nana Free.
- Nana Family monetizes network complexity and coordination.
- Nana owns the experience and trust layer while partners provide rails.
- WDK/USDT is a proven path; Arc/USDC remains an evaluation path.
