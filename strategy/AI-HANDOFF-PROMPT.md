# AI handoff prompt

Use this prompt when asking an AI collaborator to refine the Nana pitch.

---

You are helping refine Nana Wallet's external pitch deck.

First, read these files in order:

1. `strategy/MASTER-CONTEXT.md`
2. `strategy/SOURCE-OF-TRUTH.md`
3. `strategy/PITCH-REFINEMENT-BRIEF.md`
4. `pitch/PITCH-SCRIPT.md`
5. `brand/BRAND-SYSTEM.md`
6. `deck/current/Nana-Pitch-Deck-v2.pdf`
7. `mission-board/Nana-Mission-Board.pptx`

Then inspect `assets/` and `landing-reference/` before proposing visual changes.

Goal: improve the pitch's hierarchy, emotional clarity, pacing, proof and visual storytelling while preserving the product model.

Do not:

- redefine Nana as a crypto wallet for crypto users;
- make the responsible person approve every transaction;
- put core protection behind a paywall;
- present pricing, conversion, GTM or infrastructure choices as validated;
- reintroduce unsupported TAM arithmetic from the archived deck;
- expose stablecoins, chains, gas or addresses as the product experience;
- use partner infrastructure as Nana's brand identity.

The central product thesis is:

“An agentic financial account that allows people to act independently within a trusted, programmable network of relationships and permissions.”

The initial wedge is seniors and people with limited mobility.

The brand promise is:

“Independence through voice. Protection when it matters.”

Before editing, summarize:

1. what is already decided;
2. what remains a hypothesis;
3. the three weakest moments in the current deck;
4. the exact narrative and visual changes you propose.

Keep the final deck in English and suitable for investors and strategic partners.

---
