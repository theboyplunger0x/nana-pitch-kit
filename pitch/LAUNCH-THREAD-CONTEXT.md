# Launch thread context

The exact original post copy was not preserved as a standalone file. The assets and surrounding narrative establish the following thread structure. This map is included so the pitch can reuse the ideas without pretending it is a verbatim archive.

## Narrative sequence

1. **Introduce Nana.** A voice-first agentic wallet built for people excluded by financial interfaces.
2. **Personal origin.** Protecting Marcos' grandmothers gradually meant taking their independence away; one was also scammed.
3. **Accessibility insight.** Susi understood what she wanted to do, but Parkinson's made traditional app interaction difficult.
4. **Product reframe.** The goal is not fewer clicks. It is removing every interaction except meaningful human confirmation.
5. **Show the flow.** The user says what they want; Nana prepares it; the user confirms what matters. Asset: `assets/social/tweet5-flow-355KB.png`.
6. **Reveal the second user.** The senior regains independence, while the family stops running their money and hears about exceptions. Asset: `assets/social/tweet6-users-331KB.png`.
7. **Make risk concrete.** Show the scam scenario and how Nana's protection layer changes the outcome. Asset: `assets/social/tweet7-scam-1.1MB.mp4`.
8. **State the rule.** The responsible intervenes by exception, not by default.
9. **Show proof.** Seven confirmed test transactions and second place in the Tether WDK track.
10. **Explain the deeper product.** Voice is the interface; the agent is the wallet; the human retains meaningful control.
11. **Point toward the future.** Family Graph, delegated permissions and a broader agentic financial account.
12. **Close on the brand promise.** Current line: “Independence through voice. Protection when it matters.”

## Asset caveat

The historical end card says “Protection only you can provide.” That line belongs to the earlier thread iteration and should not replace the current promise. The updated pitch and landing use:

## Why this matters for the pitch

The thread found a useful order: human story → accessibility → interaction insight → dual-user model → risk → proof → vision.

That emotional order should inform the pitch even if the final slide structure changes.
