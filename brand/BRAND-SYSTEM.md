# Nana brand system for pitch work

## Brand idea

Nana should make advanced financial infrastructure feel human, calm and understandable.

The world is warm rather than clinical, protective rather than controlling, and confident rather than technical.

## Brand promise

**Independence through voice. Protection when it matters.**

## Voice principles

- Plain language before financial or technical terminology.
- Calm confidence; never patronizing.
- Human consequences before infrastructure.
- Agency before assistance.
- Protection without fear-based copy.
- Specific claims over inflated claims.

## Words we prefer

- independence;
- intent;
- trusted;
- confirm;
- protection;
- family;
- accessible;
- bounded;
- clear;
- natural.

## Words to use carefully

- elderly: prefer seniors or older adults depending on context;
- caregiver: only when the relationship is actually care-oriented;
- guardian: has legal implications;
- fraud-proof / safe / secure: avoid absolutes;
- autonomous: always explain the human control boundary;
- crypto / blockchain: infrastructure language, not the leading product message.

## Core copy

- Nana — the agentic wallet for seniors and people with limited mobility.
- Independence through voice. Protection when it matters.
- The responsible intervenes by exception, not by default.
- Manage money by expressing intent, not navigating interfaces.
- I can manage my own money.
- I don't need to control everything to know they're protected.

## Visual personality

- Warm cream and paper backgrounds.
- Nana purple used for action and confidence.
- Soft translucent lilac glass bubbles as the system motif. Use `assets/deck-textures/` for atmospheric slide backgrounds and `assets/bubbles/` only for diagrams where a bubble carries meaning.
- A very faint grid suggesting an underlying programmable system. It should recede toward the edges and never compete with copy.
- Rounded geometry and generous space.
- Translucent white/lilac panels with thin purple borders and restrained soft shadows.
- Fredoka for expressive display type; Nunito for readable body copy.
- Nani as an assistant and guide, not a mascot pasted into every composition.

## Pitch hierarchy

1. Human problem and agency.
2. Product interaction.
3. Dual-user protection model.
4. Proof.
5. Architecture and ownership.
6. Business and expansion hypotheses.

Infrastructure logos should never appear before the audience understands the human product.

## Design anti-patterns

- Generic fintech gradients with no relationship to the landing.
- Crypto imagery, coins, chains or space backgrounds.
- Dense architecture diagrams before the product is understood.
- Multiple Nani renders competing with the content.
- Tiny text used to fit every detail.
- Treating the bubbles as random decoration rather than a consistent system.
- Repeating the same small corner bubbles on every slide.
- Using mint and blush as generic decoration; reserve them for ordinary/safe versus unusual/exception states.
- Presenting hypotheses with the visual authority of facts.
