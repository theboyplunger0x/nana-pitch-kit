NANA PITCH DECK V2: CONTENT PLAN

Audience: investors, partners and early collaborators.
Language: English.
Format: 16:9, 19 slides.
Visual direction: the hybrid Nana landing with warm cream and lilac gradients, soft glass bubbles, Fredoka display typography, Nunito body, Nana product screenshots and Nani character assets. Do not use a background grid.

01 Cover: Independence through voice. Protection when it matters.
02 Problem: Protection today often means taking over.
03 Why now: The wedge is large and structurally underserved.
04 Product: Ask, prepare, confirm, settle.
05 Dual user: One account, two distinct roles.
06 Product principle: Intervention by exception, not by default.
07 Protection engine: Normal intent flows; unusual intent pauses.
08 Delegated execution: User authority, bounded and revocable agent permissions.
09 Proof: Working agentic transfer flow and hackathon validation.
10 Ownership boundary: Nana owns the experience and trust; infrastructure is modular.
11 Business model: Three revenue hypotheses, with safety never behind a paywall.
12 Wedge to platform: Seniors and mobility, then family finance, then delegated finance.
13 Market, seniors: TAM, SAM and SOM scenario for people over 75.
14 Market, limited mobility: TAM, SAM and SOM scenario for adults with hand motor impairment.
15 Market, combined: Compare both segments without adding overlapping populations.
16 Go to market: The responsible person opens the door; the end user keeps authority.
17 Built / next: Separate shipped capabilities from upcoming validation.
18 Team: Four builders and GitHub handles; no invented job titles.
19 Close: The new primitive: an agentic financial account built around trusted relationships.

Editorial rules:
- Do not present assumptions as measured facts.
- Keep Nana Family pricing explicitly marked as TBD.
- Treat Tether/WDK and Circle/Arc as infrastructure paths, not the product identity.
- Preserve the core thesis: independence for the end user, protection by exception.
- Use source notes on every slide; include official WHO sources for demographic claims.
- Label the market sizing as an internal scenario until its population inputs and geography definitions are independently validated.
