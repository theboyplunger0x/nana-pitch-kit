Communication job

By the end, the Nana team should share one language for what Nana exists to
change, what the product is, which principles are non-negotiable, and which
parts of the business model remain hypotheses.

Narrative

Mission and vision -> product architecture -> interaction -> protection rule ->
product principles -> audience and message -> visual system -> truth vs.
hypotheses -> decision filter.

Visual direction

Use the current hybrid landing system: warm cream canvas, Nana purple, soft
large spheres, a faint square grid, rounded white surfaces, strong black
rounded typography, and Nani as a calm guide rather than a childish mascot.

Fonts

The vendored artifact-tool can read and preserve fonts already embedded in an
imported PPTX, but it has no supported authoring API for embedding new font
files into a generated PPTX.
Fredoka and Nunito must be installed locally to render correctly. Get them from Google Fonts:
https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fredoka and
https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Nunito.

Open gaps

The doctrine and the shipped confirmation screen do not match. Slide 13 says
Nana is not an interface exposing rails and gas. The shipped confirmation UI
in app/apps/nana-wallet/src/routes/app.tsx, lines 345 to 360, renders amount,
destination as a raw hex address, network, and estimated cost. This is a
product decision for Marcos, so the app was not changed here.

The current slide 5 screenshots are mocked. The MSW fixture in
app/apps/nana-wallet/src/mocks/handlers.ts, lines 596 to 615, supplies 20 USDC,
the raw 0x1234567890abcdef... recipient, and base-sepolia. That conflicts with
the deck copy, which says USD-T on Ethereum Sepolia. Redo these screenshots in
live mode after the product decision is made.
